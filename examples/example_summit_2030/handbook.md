# Example Summit 2030: Volunteer Handbook

FICTIONAL DOCUMENT. Example Summit 2030 is an invented event used to demonstrate software.

## Check-in

Volunteers check in at the Blue Door of the Exampleville Convention Hall. Check-in opens 45 minutes before your first shift. Bring a printed or digital copy of your assignment.

## Clothing

Volunteer shirts are handed out at check-in. Wear closed shoes on all shifts.

## Meals

Volunteers receive one meal voucher per shift of four hours or longer. Vouchers are valid at the Hall C canteen between 11:30 and 15:00.

## Breaks

Every shift includes a 15 minute break for each two hours worked. Ask your zone lead before leaving your post.
