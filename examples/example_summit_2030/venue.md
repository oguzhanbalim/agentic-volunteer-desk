# Example Summit 2030: Venue Guide

FICTIONAL DOCUMENT. The Exampleville Convention Hall does not exist.

## Getting there

The tram stop Example Square is a five minute walk from the Blue Door. Bicycle parking is next to the Blue Door.

## Rooms

Hall A hosts the main stage. Hall B hosts workshops. Hall C holds the canteen and the volunteer lounge.

## Lost and found

Lost and found is at the information desk in the Hall A foyer. Items are kept until the final day of the event.
